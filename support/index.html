Support

Most problems have one of these three answers.

The Action Button doesn't respond

Settings → Action Button → swipe to Controls → choose Talk to Todid. Then open the app once so it can ask for microphone permission.

"That was too short to hear"

Hold the button, wait for the pill to appear, then talk. Press the button again to send immediately, or just stop talking.

A capture said it was saved for later

That happens with no connection. Open the app when you're back online — pending captures retry automatically and keep their original recording.

Still stuck?

Email rpatrianova@gmail.com with what you said and what happened.
