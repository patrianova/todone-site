To‑did

Privacy Policy

The short version: your voice goes to one AI service to be understood, and everything else stays on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing, in the analytics sense. To-did has no accounts, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and no server of its own.

What leaves your device

  • Your capture. When you record a thought, the audio (or typed text), plus your current open task titles and recent note snippets for context, is sent to Google's Gemini API to be interpreted. Google processes it under the Gemini API terms; we do not use it for anything else, and neither the audio nor the reply is stored by us anywhere off your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your tasks and notes, in the app's local database.
  • If you enable iCloud sync, they are also stored in your own iCloud account's private database, which we cannot read.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the phone itself.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you are capturing — while the recording indicator is visible — and the recording is deleted from the device after it is processed. Failed captures keep their recording locally until they can be retried, then it is deleted.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Erase all tasks and notes" removes everything, immediately. Deleting the app does the same for local data.

Children

To-did is not directed at children under 13.
