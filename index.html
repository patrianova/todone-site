It's already a to-did.

Talk the way you'd talk to a friend. To-did understands you in any language — even when you switch mid-sentence — and files what you said as a task, with the date already set.

Start talking Use it in your browser

Free for 5 captures a month. On iPhone, and in your browser with the same list.

01 · Speak

Any language, mid-sentence

Start in English, drift into Portuguese, throw in some French. It keeps up, and answers in the language you used.

02 · Filed

Dates and tags read from your words

"Amanhã às cinco" becomes tomorrow at 5:00 PM, with a reminder. "For work" files it under work. One long ramble becomes several separate tasks.

03 · Never open the app

Two taps on the back of your iPhone

Back Tap, the Action Button, Control Center or the Lock Screen widget start listening straight away. Say it, and it is on the list before you look at the phone.

04 · Yours

In your own iCloud

Your list lives in your iCloud, not in an account with us. It is there on every iPhone you sign in to, and nobody else can read it.
