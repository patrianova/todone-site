To‑done

Say it.
It's filed.

Press the Action Button on your iPhone, speak in whatever language comes out — even a mix — and To-done turns it into tasks with reminders — and answers you back. On iPhone, on your Mac (⇧⌘T), and on the web.

How it works

  • Hold the Action Button and talk. The app never has to open.
  • A ramble becomes several tasks, each with its own date and reminder.
  • "Já comprei leite" checks the milk off — matched by meaning, not exact words.
  • Ask "what's on for this week?" and the answer arrives as a notification.

Private by design

Your recording is sent to Google's Gemini API to be understood, and nothing else ever leaves your device. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. The whole policy fits on one page.

Simple pricing

Free — your list, sync across devices, the widget, and 15 voice captures a month.
To-done Pro — unlimited captures, on iPhone and Mac. $1.99/month or $14.99/year.
